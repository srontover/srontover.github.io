Research / Projects

Ongoing work across humanoid control, reinforcement learning, and motion priors.

Humanoid RLMuJoCoMJLabWarp

Learning Dynamic Skills for Humanoid Robots via Reinforcement Learning

Implemented and modified reinforcement-learning environments for T1 humanoid locomotion and contact-rich kicking tasks using MuJoCo/MJLab/Warp simulation workflows.

Problem / Goal

Build a task and training workflow that connects locomotion stability, contact timing, ball interaction, and target-oriented control.

My Contributions

  • Implemented and evaluated task components including action and observation spaces, ball reset logic, contact detection, reward functions, termination conditions, and curriculum learning procedures.
  • Implemented and evaluated reward terms and curricula for ball progress, direction, contact quality, post-kick recovery, and target distance.
  • Conducted experiments to analyze policy behavior through contact quality, ball velocity, target-direction error, termination causes, and training-stability metrics.

Outcome / Progress

The environment-to-training workflow is in place. Current iterations diagnose issues such as approaching without kicking, unstable foot selection, insufficient ball speed, and early termination; long-horizon task success remains under evaluation.

LocomotionPPOReward Design

Humanoid Locomotion Policy Training

Implemented and evaluated a simulation-based locomotion policy as a foundation for later contact-rich tasks such as kicking.

Focus

  • State observation design using joint positions and velocities, body posture, angular velocity, and commanded velocity.
  • Reward trade-offs across velocity tracking, posture stability, energy penalties, action smoothness, foot contact, and body-height constraints.
  • Policy-behavior diagnosis before transferring locomotion ability into more complex whole-body tasks.

Conditional AMPCurriculum LearningIn Progress

Conditional AMP for Generalizable Humanoid Kicking

Investigated distance-conditioned humanoid kicking with AMP-style motion priors and a curriculum over target distance and kick type.

Focus

  • Constructed condition vectors for kick type and normalized target distance, with aligned expert and policy condition distributions.
  • Implemented distance buckets, distance-dependent post-kick evaluation windows, and checkpoint support for restoring curriculum state.
  • Validated reset lifecycle and short-run stability using target-success, completion-conversion, AMP-logit, and non-finite-batch diagnostics.
Motion PriorAMPImitation Learning

Motion Prior Learning for Humanoid Skill Acquisition

Research exploration on AMASS-style human motion data, AMP, encoder-decoder policies, and motion-latent representations for humanoid skill learning.

Research Questions

  • How can human-motion data provide useful priors for natural and stable robot control?
  • How should task rewards and motion-prior rewards be balanced without reducing task completion ability?
  • How can locomotion, get-up, and kicking be represented as more continuous skills?
